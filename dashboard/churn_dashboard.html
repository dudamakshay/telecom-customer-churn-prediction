Contract Type is #1 Driver
Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7% — 15× higher than two-year contracts. Converting just 10% of M-t-M customers to annual plans saves ~$50K/month.
🔥
Fiber Optic Quality Crisis
Fiber users pay ~$91/month avg but churn at 41.9%. This signals a value-perception gap — not a pricing issue. Immediate service quality audit recommended.
🛡️
Add-Ons Reduce Churn
Customers with OnlineSecurity and TechSupport churn at significantly lower rates. Bundling these into base plans during onboarding could cut churn by ~15%.
